0004 · Supervised ML

Eighteen features

A fixed vector from physics and biology, scored by speaker-separated logistic regression or gradient boosting.

0005 · Codec crusher

Telephone realism

Clean 8 kHz, G.711 µ-law, AMR-NB, packet loss, and line noise so inspection happens under the same constraints as a call.

Focused. Scalable.

Measures reverberation decay, ambient pause floor, digital gating, and short-delay cepstral peaks. Live rooms leave tails; many clones do not.
Finds involuntary tremor, breath bursts, jitter, shimmer, and HNR. Synthetic voices often flatten micro-perturbation.
Graph attention over spectro-temporal artefacts. Primary neural vote when detection mode is pretrained or compare.
Eighteen ordered features. No speaker leaks from train into test. Falls back to baseline if the joblib model is missing.

A methodical approach to building acoustic systems.

Inputs & signals

WAV ingress, 8 kHz resample, optional G.711 / AMR crush, packet loss, and line noise before any engine sees the buffer.

Windows & direction

Six-second rolling windows, one-second hop. Physics and biology run independently, then a scorer with EMA smoothing.

Components & logic

Baseline rules, pretrained AASIST, and the 18-D ML head can run alone or side by side in compare mode.

Interfaces & verdict

Cautious labels only: leans bona-fide, leans synthetic, or inconclusive. Human verification stays in the loop.

Delivery

REST /analyze, WebSocket /stream, and this cinematic console. Streamlit remains available for lab work.
