# SonicShield Architecture

**Zero-Friction Acoustic Liveness Detection**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

SonicShield is an acoustic AI-voice-clone and liveness detection prototype designed to operate under telephone-codec conditions (8 kHz sampling rate, G.711 $\mu$-law, AMR-NB). It evaluates whether acoustic and physiological characteristics can help distinguish genuine live speech from synthetic or replayed audio without imposing active biometric friction (such as captchas or phrase repetitions) on the caller.

## 3. Engine Details

### 3.1 Physics Engine (Room and Echo)
Human telephone calls take place in physical rooms with boundaries (walls, desks, monitors, ceilings).
- **Decay Tail Slope:** When active speech ceases abruptly, real rooms reflect lingering sound energy that decays smoothly over 100–400 ms. Direct synthetic audio cutoffs drop steeply or step down instantly.
- **Room Tone in Pauses:** Natural environments possess a characteristic diffuse background room floor (typically -55 dBFS to -35 dBFS). Synthetic speech generated by neural vocoders or virtual audio cables often displays exact digital silence ($0.0$) or an artificial noise gate.
- **Cepstral Micro-Echo:** Short-delay peaks (2ms to 20ms) in the real cepstrum highlight early boundary reflections.

### 3.2 Biology Engine (Breath and Voice Variation)
Living human bodies leave involuntary physiological acoustic traces:
- **Breath Detection:** Quieter broadband noise bursts (150ms to 900ms) occurring in speech pauses, concentrated in the 1000–3500 Hz band. Natural speech exhibits approximately 10 to 22 breaths per minute.
- **Pitch Dynamics & Involuntary Tremor:** Living vocal fold phonation includes natural pitch prosody and slow 4–12 Hz micro-tremor. TTS systems frequently show unphysical flat pitch contours or robotic vocoder jumps.
- **Jitter and Shimmer:** Cycle-to-cycle perturbation measured via Praat/Parselmouth. Natural human phonation typically displays local jitter between 0.4%–2.5% and shimmer between 2%–7%. Direct neural vocoders are often artificially pristine (jitter < 0.2%, shimmer < 1.0%).
- **Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio (HNR):** Measures glottal airflow aspiration turbulence mixed with tonal harmonics.

## 4. Supervised Machine Learning Subsystem

The machine learning classifier (`src/ml_classifier.py`) evaluates windows through a trained scikit-learn pipeline:

### 4.1 Fixed 18-Dimensional Feature Schema
The feature vector maps the physical and biological properties into a fixed, immutable ordering:
1. `phys_score`
2. `phys_rms_db`
3. `phys_decay_slope`
4. `phys_reverb_score`
5. `phys_pause_rms_db`
6. `phys_spectral_flatness`
7. `phys_is_digital_silence`
8. `phys_cepstral_peak_ratio`
9. `bio_score`
10. `bio_breath_count`
11. `bio_breaths_per_min`
12. `bio_pitch_mean_hz`
13. `bio_pitch_std_hz`
14. `bio_tremor_ratio`
15. `bio_jitter_local_percent`
16. `bio_shimmer_local_percent`
17. `bio_hnr_db`
18. `bio_voiced_fraction`

### 4.2 Pipeline Architecture
```python
Pipeline([
    ("imputer", SimpleImputer(strategy="median")),
    ("scaler", StandardScaler()),
    ("clf", LogisticRegression(random_state=42, max_iter=1000, class_weight="balanced"))
])
```

### 4.3 Zero-Leakage Speaker Grouping
Training data is strictly split by unique **Speaker ID** (70% train, 15% validation, 15% test). No individual speaker appears across partitions.

## 5. Security & Privacy Considerations

- **100% Local Execution:** No audio or biometric metadata is sent to third-party cloud APIs.
- **No Identity Recognition:** SonicShield tests acoustic environmental and physiological indicators, not individual voiceprints or biometric identities.
- **Agent-Assist Paradigm:** SonicShield never terminates calls or blocks accounts automatically. Scores serve solely to equip human agents with neutral clarifying prompts.
